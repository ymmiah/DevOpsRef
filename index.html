    About DevOpsRef

    DevOpsRef is a searchable library of 214 verified academic references on DevOps and Enterprise Automation, covering CI/CD, infrastructure as code, containers and Kubernetes, DevSecOps, observability and SRE, DORA metrics, RPA and business process automation, GitOps, platform engineering, MLOps/AIOps, cloud-native architecture and more.

    Every DOI and URL was verified against Crossref, OpenAlex and publisher pages as of 26 October 2026. Citations follow Harvard (Cite Them Right) style, and every reference has a one-click copy of its in-text citation, e.g. (Forsgren, Humble and Kim, 2018), and narrative form, e.g. Forsgren, Humble and Kim (2018).
